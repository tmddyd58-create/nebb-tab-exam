## Part 1: Management & Procedural Standards (Q1 ~ Q50)

### Q1. Can a NEBB Certified Technician (CT) sign and seal a final NEBB report if the CP is out of the country?
* A) Yes, as long as the CT has more than 5 years of field experience.
* B) Yes, if the client provides written authorization.
* C) No, only a designated NEBB Certified Professional (CP) is authorized to sign and seal reports.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: C**
  * **Explanation:** Only a registered NEBB CP has the authority to sign and seal certified reports; this cannot be delegated to a CT.

### Q2. Which entity is responsible for providing the liability insurance and financial bonds required to perform TAB work on a public bid project?
* A) The NEBB Certified Firm.
* B) The NEBB National Board of Directors.
* C) The individual Certified Professional (CP).
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: A**
  * **Explanation:** Commercial insurance, bonds, and licensing are corporate liabilities managed by the Certified Firm.

### Q3. If a test kit contains three different thermometers, which ones must be listed in the report's instrument log?
* A) None, since thermometers are considered secondary tools.
* B) All thermometers used to record report data must be listed with their respective calibration dates.
* C) Only the primary thermometer needs to be listed.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** Every single temperature device that recorded official data for the report must be documented in the instrument calibration log.

### Q4. A client refuses to fix a broken damper listed in your deficiency report, but wants the final report certified. What is the CP's obligation?
* A) Alter the design flow parameters to match the actual low flow.
* B) Submit the report with the damper marked as a deficiency, noting it cannot be balanced due to owner instructions.
* C) Sign the report and omit the deficiency to please the client.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** The CP must report the truth. Unresolved items must remain in the Deficiency Log; the CP cannot falsify data or omit deficiencies.

### Q5. Who is responsible for approving the final, signed-and-sealed NEBB TAB report for building occupancy clearance?
* A) The TAB technician's firm manager.
* B) The Design Mechanical Engineer and the local building official.
* C) The Mechanical Contractor superintendent.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** The Design Engineer and building officials approve the final report to verify that design parameters are satisfied.

### Q6. A duct section leaks during testing. Who is responsible for applying duct mastic and sealing the leaks?
* A) The TAB Agent.
* B) The Controls Contractor.
* C) The Mechanical Contractor (sheet metal installer).
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: C**
  * **Explanation:** Duct sealing is a construction task and is the responsibility of the installing sheet metal/Mechanical Contractor.

### Q7. Who must provide the TAB agent with the hardware/software interface to command modulating valves and dampers?
* A) The Building Owner.
* B) The Temperature Controls Contractor (ATC).
* C) The Mechanical Contractor.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** The Controls Contractor provides the control interface, software access, and support needed to manipulate modulating dampers/valves.

### Q8. Who is responsible for verifying that the controls contractor's DDC flow readings match actual field-measured airflows?
* A) The Design Engineer.
* B) The TAB Agent (by calculating the calibration K-Factor).
* C) The Building Inspector.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** The TAB Agent measures actual physical flows and inputs correction factors (K-Factors) to align DDC readings with reality.

### Q9. Does NEBB write local municipal building codes?
* A) No, NEBB writes procedural standards that are adopted by reference in project specifications.
* B) Yes, NEBB has jurisdiction over all HVAC code enforcement.
* C) Yes, NEBB acts as the local fire department inspector.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: A**
  * **Explanation:** NEBB is a private standards body. Its standards are incorporated into contracts and specifications by reference.

### Q10. A calibration certificate does not list the instrument's serial number. How is this resolved under NEBB rules?
* A) The technician can write the serial number on the certificate in pen.
* B) The instrument cannot be used for certified reporting until a certificate with the serial number is issued.
* C) The model number is sufficient if it is unique.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** Calibration certificates must link to the specific tool using its unique serial number. Hand-written additions are invalid.

### Q11. A linear slot diffuser has a design of 400 CFM. If the measured flow is 350 CFM, is this acceptable?
* A) No, it deviates beyond the standard +/-10% range (360 to 440 CFM).
* B) Yes, because slot diffusers have wider limits.
* C) Yes, if the room pressure remains positive.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: A**
  * **Explanation:** 400 CFM * 0.9 = 360 CFM. 350 CFM is unacceptable.

### Q12. A bio-safety cabinet exhaust fan has a design airflow of 600 CFM. Under NEBB standards, what is the acceptable balanced range?
* A) 570 to 630 CFM (+/-5% of design)
* B) 600 to 660 CFM (+10% / 0% of design)
* C) 540 to 660 CFM (+/-10% of design)
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** Bio-safety containment exhaust falls under critical airflow rules and requires +10% / 0% balancing tolerance.

### Q13. Before inserting a pressure needle into a PT plug on a heating water loop, what is the best practice regarding the needle itself?
* A) Lubricate the needle with a small amount of silicone lubricant to prevent tearing the rubber core.
* B) Bend the needle slightly to bypass the core angle.
* C) Heat the needle with a lighter to sterilize it.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: A**
  * **Explanation:** Silicone lubricant protects the internal rubber core from tearing, ensuring it seals properly when the probe is removed.

### Q14. A rectangular duct is 30 inches wide by 18 inches high. What is the minimum grid layout required to satisfy the NEBB 6-inch maximum spacing rule?
* A) 5 x 3 grid (15 points)
* B) 7 x 5 grid (35 points)
* C) 6 x 4 grid (24 points)
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: C**
  * **Explanation:** Width (30/6 = 5 spaces, 6 lines), Height (18/6 = 3 spaces, 4 lines). Grid: 6 x 4.

### Q15. What is the primary indicator that a positive pressure sterile ward has lost its pressure barrier relative to the corridor?
* A) The return fan motor running current drops below no-load amps.
* B) Air flows from the corridor into the sterile ward when the door is opened.
* C) The supply air temperature falls below 55 F.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** If air flows from the corridor into the ward, the ward pressure is lower than the corridor, indicating a loss of positive pressurization.

### Q16. Why is it critical that negative pressure isolation room doors are self-closing?
* A) To reduce the cooling load on the local fan coil unit.
* B) To prevent the supply air diffusers from short-circuiting.
* C) To restore the physical airflow resistance needed to establish the target -0.05 in. w.g. static pressure barrier.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: C**
  * **Explanation:** Self-closing doors ensure the room remains sealed, allowing the system to build up the necessary static pressure difference (-0.05 in. w.g.).

### Q17. A building's return fan VFD tracks the supply fan VFD speed. If the return fan's belt slips, lowering its actual RPM, how does the building pressure respond?
* A) The building pressure becomes negative due to belt drag.
* B) The building pressure remains unchanged because VFD frequency is identical.
* C) The building pressure becomes more positive because return/exhaust volume is reduced.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: C**
  * **Explanation:** Lower return fan RPM reduces exhaust/return airflow, causing supply air to accumulate and increasing positive building pressure.

### Q18. A VAV system has multiple branches. How does the DDC system determine the static pressure setpoint if it uses static pressure reset logic?
* A) It polls all VAV boxes and resets the setpoint to keep at least one box damper 90% to 95% open.
* B) It maintains a fixed setpoint based on the fan's maximum RPM.
* C) It averages the inlet damper angles and multiplies by the fan frequency.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: A**
  * **Explanation:** Static pressure reset logic continuously lowers the static setpoint until at least one VAV box damper is almost fully open, minimizing fan energy.

### Q19. If a static pressure sensor tip is angled at 45 degrees relative to the airflow direction in a VAV duct, how will the reading be affected?
* A) The sensor will read a combination of static and velocity pressure, resulting in an inaccurate reading.
* B) The reading will be unaffected because static pressure is omnidirectional.
* C) The reading will drop to zero due to boundary layer separation.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: A**
  * **Explanation:** Static pressure probes must be perpendicular to the flow. Angling the probe allows velocity pressure to impact the sensor, skewing the static reading.

### Q20. In a VAV bypass relief setup, why is returning the relief air to the return air plenum preferred over venting it outside?
* A) To reduce the physical noise of the bypass air.
* B) To conserve the thermal energy (heating/cooling) of the conditioned supply air.
* C) To prevent building over-pressurization.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** Returning bypassed supply air to the return plenum saves energy by recycling already conditioned (cooled or heated) air.
