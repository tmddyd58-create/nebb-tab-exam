# NEBB TAB CP Exam Prep Set 5 (English 100-Question)

This is the practice exam **Set 5** focusing on certified study requirements. Every question is 3-choice (A, B, C) with full answers and explanations.

## Part 1: (Q1 ~ Q50)

### Q1. If a dispute arises under the NEBB Quality Assurance Program (QAP) and re-balancing work is ordered, which entity bears the financial responsibility for the re-work?
* A) The local NEBB chapter office.
* B) The individual Certified Professional (CP) who signed the report.
* C) The NEBB Certified Firm.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: C**
  * **Explanation:** The NEBB Certified Firm (the corporate entity) provides the financial and quality guarantees under the QAP contract.

### Q2. During field testing, you find that a key volume damper has detached from its linkage inside a duct. Who is contractually responsible for the physical repair?
* A) The Mechanical Contractor.
* B) The TAB Certified Professional.
* C) The building commissioning engineer.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: A**
  * **Explanation:** TAB agents only diagnose and report. The physical repair of mechanical system components is the responsibility of the Mechanical Contractor.

### Q3. Under NEBB standards, what details must be included in the Instrument Calibration Log of the final certified report?
* A) The purchasing invoices and rental receipts for all field tools.
* B) The manufacturer, model, serial number, and exact calibration date of each instrument used.
* C) The technician's certification registration number.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** The calibration log must list manufacturer, model, serial number, and calibration date to prove the instruments were within the 12-month limit.

### Q4. A Certified Firm's sole NEBB Certified Professional (CP) resigns. Under NEBB bylaws, what must the firm do to prevent automatic suspension of its certification?
* A) Deregister the firm and operate under a local engineering license.
* B) Allow the Certified Technician (CT) to sign and seal reports indefinitely.
* C) Notify NEBB immediately and replace the CP within the designated grace period (typically 30 days).
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: C**
  * **Explanation:** Firms must employ at least one full-time CP. Loss of the sole CP requires immediate notification and replacement within the grace period (usually 30 days).
