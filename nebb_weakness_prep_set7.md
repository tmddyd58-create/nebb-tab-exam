## Part 1: Management & Procedural Standards (Q1 ~ Q50)

### Q1. If a certified report is found to contain falsified data, who is subject to disciplinary action by the NEBB Ethics Committee?
* A) The building mechanical contractor superintendent.
* B) The entire engineering design team.
* C) The individual Certified Professional (CP) who signed and sealed the report.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: C**
  * **Explanation:** Ethical violations and data falsification hold the individual CP who signed and sealed the report personally accountable.

### Q2. What is the consequence to the Certified Firm if its only employed CP resigns from the company?
* A) The firm can continue operating under a CT's supervision indefinitely.
* B) The firm's certification is suspended if they do not replace the CP within the designated grace period (typically 30 days).
* C) The firm must refund the cost of all active contracts.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** A Certified Firm must employ at least one full-time CP. Loss of the sole CP triggers suspension if not replaced within the grace period.

### Q3. An instrument log is missing the serial number of a digital micromanometer. What is the status of the report?
* A) It is fully valid as long as the model number is listed.
* B) It is non-compliant with NEBB standards and must be corrected before certification.
* C) It is acceptable if the CP verbally verifies the serial number.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** All instrument identifiers (including serial numbers) are mandatory data fields under NEBB Procedural Standards.

### Q4. Who must receive copies of the intermediate Deficiency Reports as they are generated in the field?
* A) The Mechanical Contractor, Design Engineer, and Client Representative.
* B) The local electrical utility provider.
* C) The NEBB National Office.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: A**
  * **Explanation:** Immediate stakeholders (Mechanical Contractor, Engineer, and Client) must receive reports so corrective actions can be scheduled.

### Q5. If a duct system has insufficient straight duct for a traverse, who must approve a deviation from standard traverse criteria?
* A) The TAB technician in the field.
* B) The sheet metal installer.
* C) The Design Mechanical Engineer.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: C**
  * **Explanation:** Any deviation from standard design layouts or traverse criteria must be reviewed and approved by the Design Engineer.

### Q6. Who must install the manual volume control dampers (VCDs) and balancing valves (CBVs) in the duct and pipe runs?
* A) The Building Owner.
* B) The TAB Agent.
* C) The Mechanical Contractor.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: C**
  * **Explanation:** The installation of all balancing dampers, valves, and physical fittings is the sole duty of the Mechanical Contractor.

### Q7. If a VAV box damper actuator is stroking in the wrong direction, who must reprogram the actuator polarity?
* A) The Temperature Controls Contractor (ATC).
* B) The TAB Agent.
* C) The Design Engineer.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: A**
  * **Explanation:** Actuator direction and DDC control polarity configuration belong to the Controls Contractor.

### Q8. If a system is balanced but cannot meet design flow, what must the TAB Agent do?
* A) Document the maximum attainable flows and issue a Deficiency Report explaining the limit.
* B) Falsify the report values to match design parameters.
* C) Re-pipe the pump suction line to reduce pressure drop.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: A**
  * **Explanation:** The TAB Agent must report actual measured data and issue a Deficiency Report outlining the performance limits.

### Q9. If a certified firm is found to have breached NEBB standards, what is NEBB's institutional role in the dispute?
* A) To replace the mechanical design engineer of the building.
* B) To pay for the owner's legal fees in court.
* C) To investigate the QAP complaint and, if necessary, order the firm to perform corrective work at no cost to the owner.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: C**
  * **Explanation:** Under the QAP, NEBB mediates disputes, audits reports, and can compel the Certified Firm to perform corrective work.

### Q10. Who must review the calibration certificates of all tools before they are used on a certified project?
* A) The Sheet Metal Installer.
* B) The NEBB Certified Professional (CP).
* C) The Controls Contractor.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** The CP is responsible for verifying that all field equipment used by the firm is certified and calibrated.

### Q11. An exhaust register has a design rate of 200 CFM. If the measured flow is 215 CFM, is this acceptable?
* A) No, it exceeds the design rate.
* B) Yes, it is within the standard +/-10% range (180 to 220 CFM).
* C) Yes, because exhaust systems have a +/-20% tolerance.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** 200 CFM * 1.1 = 220 CFM. 215 CFM is acceptable.

### Q12. An outside air intake damper for a positive pressure building is rated for 2000 CFM. What is the acceptable balanced range?
* A) 1800 to 2200 CFM (+/-10% of design)
* B) 2000 to 2200 CFM (+10% / 0% of design)
* C) 2000 to 2400 CFM (+20% / 0% of design)
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** Outside air intake must be at or above design (+10% / 0%) to guarantee the building maintains positive pressure.

### Q13. What is the primary physical hazard when removing a PT plug's metal protective cap from a pressurized steam condensate line?
* A) The cap can be launched by trapped pressurized hot liquid/steam if the plug core is leaking.
* B) Electrocution from local static buildup.
* C) Duct collapse due to sudden vacuum drop.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: A**
  * **Explanation:** If the internal core leaks, pressure builds under the cap. Removing it can spray hot liquid/steam or launch the cap.

### Q14. A rectangular duct is 36 inches wide by 12 inches high. What is the minimum grid layout required to satisfy the NEBB 6-inch maximum spacing rule?
* A) 7 x 3 grid (21 points)
* B) 6 x 2 grid (12 points)
* C) 6 x 3 grid (18 points)
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: A**
  * **Explanation:** Width (36/6 = 6 spaces, 7 lines), Height (12/6 = 2 spaces, 3 lines). Grid: 7 x 3.

### Q15. For a sterile compounding pharmacy cleanroom, which airflow configuration ensures proper positive pressure hierarchy?
* A) Exhaust airflow exceeds supply airflow.
* B) Supply airflow equals return airflow with no exhaust.
* C) Supply airflow exceeds the sum of return and exhaust airflows.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: C**
  * **Explanation:** Supply air must exceed return + exhaust to create a net outward flow, which generates positive pressurization.

### Q16. A differential pressure monitor on a negative pressure ward reads 0.00 in. w.g. because the door is open. How should the DDC controls respond if they track flow rather than pressure?
* A) Shut down the exhaust fan to prevent motor overload.
* B) Increase supply fan speed to try to restore the pressure differential.
* C) Maintain constant exhaust flow to ensure inward velocity across the door opening.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: C**
  * **Explanation:** Flow-tracking control systems maintain constant volumetric flow (exhaust > supply) to guarantee inward velocity when barriers are breached.

### Q17. If a return air fan speed is controlled to track the supply fan speed with a constant offset of 1000 CFM (Supply > Return), what happens if the return fan VFD fails and stops?
* A) The supply fan will automatically ramp down to 0 CFM.
* B) The building will fall negative, drawing in unconditioned outside air.
* C) The building will become highly positive, causing doors to stand open.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: C**
  * **Explanation:** If the return fan stops while the supply fan continues, exhaust/return is cut, leading to building over-pressurization.

### Q18. If a static pressure sensor is placed too close to the fan discharge, what will be the effect on the VAV box dampers closest to the end of the run?
* A) They will automatically lock in the fully closed position.
* B) They will be subject to excessive pressure, causing noisy air discharge.
* C) They may experience insufficient static pressure during peak load conditions, leading to under-airflow.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: C**
  * **Explanation:** A sensor near the fan discharge does not account for downstream duct friction losses. At peak loads, the end-of-run pressure drops, starving terminal boxes.

### Q19. To obtain a clean static pressure signal for VAV VFD control, how much straight duct is recommended before the sensor probe?
* A) No straight duct is needed if a static pressure grid is used.
* B) At least 10 duct diameters of straight duct upstream.
* C) At least 2 duct diameters upstream and downstream.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** To avoid turbulence and ensure a laminar flow profile, the sensor should be placed in a straight run of duct, ideally 10 duct diameters downstream of disturbances.

### Q20. A bypass relief damper controller is set to maintain duct static pressure. If the damper actuator fails in the closed position, what happens at low VAV loads?
* A) Duct static pressure will spike, and the supply fan will enter a surge condition.
* B) The room temperatures will drop rapidly.
* C) The supply fan will automatically speed up to compensate.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: A**
  * **Explanation:** With the bypass closed and VAV boxes shut, static pressure will rise unchecked, forcing the fan into its unstable surge region.

  * **Explanation:** Negative pressure in operating theater Room 210 is maintained by ensuring exhaust volume is greater than supply volume.
