## Part 1: (Q1 ~ Q50)

### Q1. At an altitude of 3000 feet, the air density correction factor is 0.91. If the measured velocity pressure (VP) is 0.25 in. w.g., what is the actual corrected air velocity?
* A) 2099 FPM
* B) 2518 FPM
* C) 1679 FPM
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: A**
  * **Explanation:** Using corrected formula V = 4005 * sqrt(VP / df). V = 4005 * sqrt(0.25 / 0.91) = 2099 FPM.

### Q2. A kitchen grease exhaust duct has dimensions of 12 inches by 13 inches. If the velocity is measured at 1505 FPM, what is the total exhaust airflow?
* A) 2119 CFM
* B) 1630 CFM
* C) 1141 CFM
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** Using Q = V * A. Duct area A = (12 * 13) / 144 = 1.083 sq.ft. Q = 1505 * 1.083 = 1630 CFM.

### Q3. A 3-phase motor has measured line-to-line voltages of L1-L2 = 452V, L2-L3 = 462V, and L3-L1 = 453V. What is the voltage unbalance percentage?
* A) 1.39%
* B) 0.69%
* C) 2.08%
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: A**
  * **Explanation:** Average voltage = (452+462+453)/3 = 455.7V. Maximum deviation = 6.3V. VU% = (6.3 / 455.7) * 100 = 1.39%.

### Q4. An underfloor air distribution (UFAD) plenum operates at a low static pressure. If the measured plenum pressure is 0.053 in. w.g., is this within the standard design range for UFAD systems?
* A) No, it must exceed 1.0 in. w.g. to avoid system starvations.
* B) Yes, but only if the supply water temperature is above 60 F.
* C) Yes, UFAD plenums typically operate between 0.05 and 0.10 in. w.g.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: C**
  * **Explanation:** UFAD systems use the floor cavity as a pressurized plenum, operating at very low static pressure (0.05 to 0.10 in. w.g.) to prevent drafts.
