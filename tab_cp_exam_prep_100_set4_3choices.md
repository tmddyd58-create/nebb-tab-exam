## Part 1: (Q1 ~ Q50)

### Q1. During freezing winter operation, a building's freezestat trips repeatedly despite the average mixed air temperature being 45 F. What field troubleshooting action is required?
* A) Replace the heating coil valve actuator with a higher torque unit.
* B) Check for air stratification in the mixing box and install air blenders or adjust dampers.
* C) Bypass the freezestat sensor loop in the DDC software permanently.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** Air stratification creates cold air pockets (e.g., 20 F) that freeze localized coil tubes, tripping the freezestat even when the average temperature is safe.

### Q2. A VAV fan VFD is at its minimum limit of 15Hz, but the main duct static pressure continues to spike, causing the fan to enter a pulsing, noisy stall region. What mechanical defect is likely?
* A) The static bypass relief damper actuator is stuck closed or is disconnected.
* B) The return fan motor has run in reverse polarity.
* C) The supply fan pulley is running at twice its rated RPM.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: A**
  * **Explanation:** If terminal boxes close and the VFD is at minimum speed, static pressure spikes. A closed bypass relief damper forces the fan into its unstable surging region.

### Q3. In a primary-secondary chilled water system, the secondary loop supply water temperature rises to 49 F, while the chiller discharge is steady at 45 F. What does this hydronic mismatch indicate?
* A) The primary pump has experienced impeller cavitation.
* B) Secondary flow exceeds primary flow, forcing warm return water to flow backward through the decoupler into secondary suction.
* C) The expansion tank pressure has bled to zero.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** When secondary GPM is greater than primary GPM, the deficit is filled by secondary return water bypassing backward through the decoupler, raising supply water temperature.

### Q4. A modulating cooling coil valve opens slightly but immediately floods the coil with water, causing the space temperature to oscillate (hunt) rapidly. What is the root cause of this loop instability?
* A) The chilled water pump is running at excessive speed.
* B) The valve actuator stroke range is programmed backward.
* C) Low valve authority, meaning the control valve is oversized and acts like an on/off valve.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: C**
  * **Explanation:** Oversized valves have low valve authority. They cannot modulate flow linearly, causing excessive flow at low openings, which leads to loop hunting.
