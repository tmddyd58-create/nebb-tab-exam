## Part 1: Management & Procedural Standards (Q1 ~ Q15)

### Q1. According to NEBB Procedural Standards, what is the maximum allowable calibration interval for instruments used to measure airflow and hydronic differential pressure in the field?
* A) 6 months
* B) 12 months
* C) 24 months
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** All testing instruments used to generate reported data must be calibrated every **12 months (1 year)**.

### Q2. While conducting a hydronic test, a technician notices that the digital manometer's calibration certificate expires in two weeks, but the sensor has developed a zero-point drift. What is the correct procedure?
* A) Continue using the meter until the two weeks expire, then send it for calibration.
* B) Remove the meter from service immediately, use a backup, and send the drifting meter for recalibration.
* C) Adjust all subsequent readings by applying a manual offset correction factor.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** Any instrument suspected of being out of calibration or experiencing drift must be **withdrawn from service immediately** to protect the integrity of the data.

### Q3. Under NEBB standards, what is the standard engineering tolerance for individual air terminal grilles, registers, and diffusers (GRDs) relative to their design airflow?
* A) $\pm 5\%$
* B) $\pm 10\%$
* C) $\pm 20\%$
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** Standard NEBB tolerances for individual air and hydronic terminal devices are **$\pm 10\%$** of the design flow rate.

### Q4. To maintain positive building pressure hierarchy, what is the standard balancing tolerance for critical outside air (OA) intake rates?
* A) $\pm 10\%$ of design
* B) $+10\% / 0\%$ of design
* C) $\pm 20\%$ of design
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** Flow rates critical to maintaining space pressurization relationships (such as outside air intake or containment exhaust) must be set at **design value or higher (+10% / 0%)** to prevent unintended reverse pressurization.

### Q5. During balancing, a TAB technician discovers that a major variable frequency drive (VFD) is bypassed and the fan is running at a constant 60Hz. Who bears the primary responsibility for physically repairing the DDC interface to allow variable speed operation?
* A) The TAB Certified Professional (CP)
* B) The Mechanical Contractor or DDC Controls Contractor
* C) The Building Owner's Representative
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** The TAB agent's role is to **identify and report** the deficiency. Corrective action (physical repairs, controls configuration, etc.) is the responsibility of the **Mechanical/Controls Contractor**.

### Q6. Which of the following information is NOT a mandatory data field for a pump performance report under NEBB reporting standards?
* A) Actual measured line-to-line voltages and phase amperages
* B) The wholesale purchase cost of the pump and motor assembly
* C) The manufacturer's nameplate Full Load Amps (FLA) rating
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** Financial costs are irrelevant to technical reporting. Voltage, operating amperages, and nameplate data are critical for assessing motor overload.

### Q7. When preparing to test a high-temperature hot water balancing valve using a PT plug, what is the most critical safety precaution?
* A) Turn off all lighting in the mechanical room.
* B) Wear safety glasses and heat-resistant gloves before removing the protective cap.
* C) Completely isolate the main building bypass loop.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** PT plugs operate under system pressure. Safety glasses and gloves protect the technician from sudden hot water/steam spray during probe insertion.

### Q8. What does "NIST traceability" on an instrument's calibration certificate legally verify?
* A) That the instrument was manufactured in the United States.
* B) That the instrument's calibration path is linked to the national physical standards maintained by NIST.
* C) That the instrument is certified to be maintenance-free for five years.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** Traceability to **NIST** ensures that the calibration accuracy is verifiable against the ultimate national physical reference standards.

### Q9. During a test, a fire damper fusible link is found broken, causing the blade to fall and block 90% of the duct. What immediate administrative action must the TAB agent take?
* A) Order a replacement fusible link and install it themselves.
* B) Issue a formal Deficiency Report to the contractor/owner and suspend balancing in that duct section.
* C) Note the issue in the final report draft without notifying the contractor in the field.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** The TAB team must immediately report physical system blockages that impede air balancing to the contractor via a **Deficiency Report** so repairs can be scheduled.

### Q10. What is the NEBB required minimum number of traverse points for a rectangular duct that is 18" wide and 12" high, assuming a 3x3 grid does not exceed maximum spacing rules?
* A) 9 points
* B) 16 points (4x4 grid)
* C) 25 points
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** NEBB rectangular duct traverse rules state that grid spacing must not exceed 6 inches. An 18" width requires at least 4 divisions, and a 12" height requires at least 4 divisions, resulting in a **4x4 grid (16 points)**.

### Q11. While executing LOTO (Lockout/Tagout) on an exhaust fan, who is authorized to remove the safety lock and tag from the electrical disconnect switch?
* A) The building manager.
* B) Any licensed electrician on site.
* C) The specific technician who applied the lock and tag.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: C**
  * **Explanation:** Under OSHA and NEBB safety rules, the lock/tag can only be removed by **the individual who applied it**, ensuring no one starts the machine while another worker is still inside.

### Q12. Under NEBB guidelines, what must be completed on the hydronic side before any balancing valve adjustments are made?
* A) The system must be fully vented of air, and all strainers must be checked and cleaned.
* B) All control valves must be locked in the 50% open position.
* C) The expansion tank air bladder pressure must be completely bled off.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: A**
  * **Explanation:** Air pockets and blocked strainers alter system hydraulics. **Air venting and strainer cleaning** are essential pre-requisites for accurate water balancing.

### Q13. If a project specification requires duct leakage testing to verify seal class compliance, which standards govern the test setup and maximum allowable leakage rates?
* A) SMACNA Duct Leakage Test Standards
* B) AMCA Fan Performance Standards
* C) ASME Boiler Code
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: A**
  * **Explanation:** SMACNA standards dictate duct construction, sealing classes, and leakage testing methods.

### Q14. When submitting a final NEBB certified report, whose signature and seal are required to bind the document's validity?
* A) The design mechanical engineer's seal.
* B) The NEBB Certified Professional (CP) employed by the TAB firm.
* C) The general contractor's superintendent.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** The report must be signed and sealed by the **NEBB Certified Professional** who oversaw the project to certify that the data was obtained in compliance with NEBB standards.

### Q15. A client files a dispute claiming the balanced flow rates on a project are inaccurate. Under the NEBB Quality Assurance Program (QAP), what is the time limit for the owner to submit a formal complaint?
* A) Within 12 months (1 year) of report submission.
* B) Within 3 years.
* C) Up to 5 years.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: A**
  * **Explanation:** The NEBB QAP coverage limit for disputes is **1 year** from the date of final report approval.

## Part 2: System Building Relationships (Q16 ~ Q30)

### Q16. Why must a sterile surgical operating room be kept under a positive pressure relationship relative to its adjacent corridor?
* A) To prevent the exhaust fan motor from overheating.
* B) To prevent airborne contaminants and dust from corridor air from infiltrating the sterile environment.
* C) To increase the dehumidification capacity of the local cooling coil.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** Positive pressure forces air outward, preventing **infiltration** of dirty corridor air into the sterile surgery space.

### Q17. A negative-pressure isolation room is monitored at a differential pressure of -0.05 inches w.g. When the sliding entrance door is opened, what happens to the differential pressure and the airflow direction?
* A) The pressure drop decreases to nearly 0 w.g., but the airflow direction remains inward from corridor to room.
* B) The pressure drop spikes to -0.50 inches w.g. due to sudden expansion.
* C) The airflow direction reverses, pushing air out into the corridor.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: A**
  * **Explanation:** Opening the door removes the physical restriction, causing the static pressure difference to drop to **near zero**. However, the continuous exhaust pulls air in, maintaining **inward airflow direction** (containment).

### Q18. If a building's Return Air Fan (RF) speed controller fails and locks the fan at 100% capacity while the Supply Air Fan (SF) is running at 70% capacity, what envelope condition is created?
* A) A positive envelope pressure, causing exterior doors to fly open.
* B) A negative envelope pressure, causing exterior doors to stick and raw outdoor air to infiltrate.
* C) Zero envelope pressure difference due to loop cancellation.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** Excess exhaust/return air relative to supply air creates a **net negative building pressure**, pulling unconditioned outdoor air through the building envelope.

### Q19. In a variable air volume (VAV) system, where is the duct static pressure sensor for fan speed control typically located to ensure system-wide pressure balance?
* A) Directly at the supply fan discharge plenum.
* B) Approximately 2/3 to 3/4 of the way down the main duct trunk.
* C) At the inlet of the return air fan.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** Placing the sensor **2/3 to 3/4 down the main duct** ensures that the furthest VAV boxes have enough pressure to operate without over-pressurizing the beginning of the run.

### Q20. If a static pressure sensor is mistakenly installed immediately downstream of the supply fan discharge, what control problem is likely to occur?
* A) The sensor will read zero static pressure due to high elevation.
* B) High velocity pressure fluctuations and turbulence will cause the VFD to constantly hunt and oscillate.
* C) The fan will automatically reverse its rotation.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** The discharge of the fan is a region of high turbulence and high velocity pressure. The sensor will capture dynamic pressure noise, causing **control loop hunting**.

### Q21. A VAV system is operating at minimum load. The fan VFD is at its minimum limit of 15Hz, but the static pressure continues to rise past the setpoint, causing the fan to enter a pulsing, noisy surge region. What design feature is missing or malfunctioning?
* A) A bypass relief damper connecting the supply air main back to the return plenum.
* B) An outdoor air pre-heater.
* C) A pump impeller trim.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: A**
  * **Explanation:** A **Bypass Relief Damper** bypasses excess air to the return side when system flow demand drops below the fan's minimum aerodynamic stability limit, preventing surging.

### Q22. In a primary-secondary hydronic system, what is the ideal pressure drop across the common pipe (decoupler) connecting the loops?
* A) Equal to the pump's total dynamic head (TDH).
* B) Nearly 0 psi (0 feet of head).
* C) At least 50 psi to force flow separation.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** To ensure that the primary and secondary pumps do not dynamically interfere with each other, the decoupler pipe must have **zero pressure drop (frictionless bypass)**.

### Q23. If the cold deck and hot deck dampers of a dual-duct mixing box open 100% simultaneously due to a control system malfunction, what is the primary consequence?
* A) The space temperature will reach absolute equilibrium.
* B) Extreme mixing losses occur, overloading both the chiller and boiler, and wasting massive amounts of energy.
* C) The fan will experience vacuum cavitation.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** Simultaneous full heating and full cooling neutralize each other, causing **high mixing energy losses** and severe central plant overload.

### Q24. When a DDC controller detects a cut wire (open circuit) on an NTC thermistor temperature sensor, how does the controller interpret the signal and what action does it take?
* A) It interprets infinite resistance as an extremely low temperature, potentially commanding the heating valve to open 100%.
* B) It interprets zero resistance as a high temperature, closing all heating valves.
* C) It ignores the reading and averages other sensors.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: A**
  * **Explanation:** In NTC sensors, resistance increases as temperature drops. An open circuit represents **infinite resistance**, which the DDC interprets as **sub-zero cold**, triggering full heating.

### Q25. Why does wintertime air stratification occur inside an air handling unit mixing chamber?
* A) Warm return air and freezing outdoor air do not mix properly due to lack of baffle plates or mixing devices, causing distinct temperature layers.
* B) The supply fan speed is too high.
* C) The chilled water density is too high.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: A**
  * **Explanation:** Stratification is the layering of cold and warm air streams, which can cause freezing air to strike a heating coil and trip the **freezestat**.

### Q26. If the valve authority ($a$) of a 2-way modulating temperature control valve is too low (e.g., $a = 0.05$), how does it affect the temperature control loop?
* A) The valve will not open at all.
* B) The valve behaves as an on-off valve, causing rapid room temperature oscillations (hunting) at low openings.
* C) The water flow rate remains constant regardless of valve position.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** Low valve authority distorts the valve's control curve, making it act like an **on-off switch** near the closed position, which leads to **loop hunting**.

### Q27. A multi-zone office building suffers from exterior doors that are difficult to close, accompanied by whistling noises around the door frames. What is the most likely system relationship cause?
* A) The return fan is running too fast.
* B) The building is over-pressurized due to excessive outdoor air intake or inadequate exhaust/return fan tracking.
* C) The supply duct static pressure sensor has failed open.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** High positive pressure inside the building pushes doors open and causes **whistling air leaks** through door seals.

### Q28. A secondary loop variable-volume pump is tracking a remote differential pressure sensor. If the sensor wire is shorted (zero resistance), causing the DDC to read 0 psi differential pressure, how will the pump VFD respond?
* A) The pump VFD will ramp up to 100% speed (maximum frequency) in an attempt to raise the pressure to the setpoint.
* B) The pump VFD will shut down immediately to prevent damage.
* C) The pump will reverse its direction of rotation.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: A**
  * **Explanation:** Seeing a 0 psi differential pressure feedback, the control loop will try to correct the perceived "zero pressure" by **ramping the VFD to 100% speed**.

### Q29. When a secondary loop VAV pump draws more water than the primary loop constant-speed pump is producing, what is the impact on the common decoupler pipe?
* A) Secondary return water bypasses backward through the decoupler into the secondary pump suction, raising the supply water temperature.
* B) The decoupler pipe collapses due to high vacuum.
* C) The primary pump motor immediately trips on overload.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: A**
  * **Explanation:** When secondary demand exceeds primary supply, warm return water must flow **backward through the decoupler** to balance the secondary pump suction flow, raising the supply water temperature.

### Q30. A pressure-independent VAV terminal box uses a flow inlet ring sensor. If the tubing connecting the sensor to the DDC controller is disconnected in the field, what will the VAV box do?
* A) The box will close its damper completely (0% open).
* B) The box will modulate to its midpoint.
* C) The box will drive its damper 100% open because it senses 0 CFM of flow and tries to meet the minimum flow setpoint.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: C**
  * **Explanation:** The disconnected tubing prevents the sensor from reading velocity pressure. The DDC registers **0 CFM flow** and drives the **damper 100% open** to try to reach the target flow rate.
