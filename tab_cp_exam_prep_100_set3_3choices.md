## Part 1: (Q1 ~ Q50)

### Q1. A cooling coil handles 4000 CFM. The air enthalpy drop is 9.0 Btu/lb, and the dry-bulb temperature drop is 25 F. What is the total heat rejection rate (trap: do not use the sensible heat formula for total heat)?
* A) 210,600 Btu/hr
* B) 108,000 Btu/hr
* C) 162,000 Btu/hr
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: C**
  * **Explanation:** Total heat Q_t = 4.5 * CFM * dh. Q_t = 4.5 * 4000 * 9.0 = 162,000 Btu/hr. (Q_s = 1.08 * CFM * dT is sensible heat only).

### Q2. A pump delivers 201 GPM at 61 feet of head with a mechanical efficiency of 75% (0.75). What is the motor brake horsepower (BHP) required (trap: do not confuse WHP and BHP)?
* A) 5.37 BHP
* B) 4.13 BHP (BHP)
* C) 3.10 BHP (WHP)
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** WHP = (GPM * H) / 3960 = (201 * 61) / 3960 = 3.10 HP. BHP = WHP / efficiency = 3.10 / 0.75 = 4.13 BHP.

### Q3. A multi-stage pump impeller is trimmed from 12.0 inches to 11.4 inches. If the initial flow rate is 102 GPM, what is the new flow rate (trap: multi-stage design trims all stages equally, and flow scales to the 1st power of diameter ratio)?
* A) 96.9 GPM
* B) 92.1 GPM
* C) 87.5 GPM
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: A**
  * **Explanation:** Flow rate scales directly to diameter ratio (1st power): GPM2 = GPM1 * (D2/D1). GPM2 = 102 * (11.4/12.0) = 96.9 GPM.

### Q4. An orifice plate has a measured differential pressure drop of 4.0 psi. If the permanent pressure loss coefficient is 63%, what is the permanent pressure loss in the loop?
