### Q1. Under standard air conditions, if the measured velocity pressure (VP) in a supply duct is 0.04 in. w.g., what is the calculated velocity?
* A) 801 FPM
* B) 961 FPM
* C) 640 FPM
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: A**
  * **Explanation:** Using formula V = 4005 * sqrt(VP). V = 4005 * sqrt(0.04) = 801 FPM.

### Q2. A centrifugal supply fan operates at 801 RPM delivering 5020 CFM. If the fan speed is increased to 921 RPM, what is the new airflow?
* A) 7504 CFM
* B) 5773 CFM
* C) 4041 CFM
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** Using Fan Law 1: CFM2 = CFM1 * (RPM2/RPM1). CFM2 = 5020 * (921/801) = 5773 CFM.

### Q3. A chilled water coil has a water flow rate of 22 GPM and an entering/leaving water temperature difference of 12 F. What is the heat rejection rate?
* A) 158,400 Btu/hr
* B) 105,600 Btu/hr
* C) 132,000 Btu/hr
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: C**
  * **Explanation:** Using formula Q = 500 * GPM * dT. Q = 500 * 22 * 12 = 132,000 Btu/hr.

### Q4. A 3-phase motor runs on 460V and draws 18A. If the motor power factor is 0.85 and efficiency is 90% (0.90), what is the calculated brake horsepower (BHP)?
* A) 19.12 BHP
* B) 14.71 BHP
* C) 10.3 BHP
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** Using formula BHP = (V * I * 1.732 * PF * Eff) / 746. BHP = (460 * 18 * 1.732 * 0.85 * 0.90) / 746 = 14.71 BHP.
