## Part 1: Management & Procedural Standards (Q1 ~ Q50)

### Q1. Who is contractually and professionally responsible for applying the physical NEBB seal and original signature to the final certified TAB report?
* A) The client's commissioning authority.
* B) The president of the NEBB Certified Firm.
* C) The designated NEBB Certified Professional (CP) overseeing the project.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: C**
  * **Explanation:** Applying the seal and signature is the personal professional responsibility of the individual NEBB Certified Professional (CP).

### Q2. If a dispute arises under the NEBB Quality Assurance Program (QAP) and re-balancing work is ordered, which entity bears the financial responsibility for the re-work?
* A) The NEBB Certified Firm.
* B) The NEBB Local Chapter.
* C) The individual Certified Professional (CP).
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: A**
  * **Explanation:** The Certified Firm (the business entity) bears the financial and contractual responsibility under the QAP guarantee.

### Q3. Which of the following must be included in the Instrument Calibration Log of a certified NEBB TAB report?
* A) The purchase price and rental agreement for each instrument.
* B) The home address of the technician who owns the instrument.
* C) The manufacturer, model, serial number, and exact calibration date of each instrument used.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: C**
  * **Explanation:** NEBB requires the model, serial number, and exact calibration date to prove the instruments were within the 12-month calibration limit.

### Q4. What is the mandatory function of the Deficiency Report Log in a final NEBB certified report?
* A) To list all mechanical, electrical, and control system defects that remain unresolved at the conclusion of TAB.
* B) To record the names of technicians who arrived late to the jobsite.
* C) To log the contractor's billing disputes with the owner.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: A**
  * **Explanation:** The Deficiency Log details unresolved system problems that prevent the systems from meeting design parameters.

### Q5. Which project stakeholder is contractually responsible for establishing the design airflow (CFM) and water flow (GPM) parameters?
* A) The Mechanical Contractor.
* B) The Design Mechanical Engineer.
* C) The TAB Certified Professional.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** The Design Engineer calculates the building loads and establishes all design flow and pressure requirements.

### Q6. Who is responsible for cleaning all hydronic strainers before the water balancing process begins?
* A) The TAB Certified Professional.
* B) The Mechanical Contractor.
* C) The Building Maintenance Engineer.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** Strainer cleaning is a mechanical installation/maintenance task, which is the responsibility of the Mechanical Contractor.

### Q7. Who is responsible for calibrating the local DDC flow sensors and air terminal box controllers?
* A) The Design Engineer.
* B) The TAB Agent.
* C) The Temperature Controls Contractor (ATC).
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: C**
  * **Explanation:** DDC sensor calibration and controller programming are the contractual responsibility of the Controls Contractor.

### Q8. What is the primary contractual role of the TAB Agent on a commercial HVAC project?
* A) To rewrite the control algorithms of the DDC system.
* B) To install ductwork and piping runs.
* C) To measure, adjust, and balance air and water flows to design specifications, and report deviations.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: C**
  * **Explanation:** The TAB agent's role is strictly to test, adjust, and balance existing systems and document the results.

### Q9. What is the primary institutional purpose of NEBB as an organization?
* A) To establish procedural standards, certify firms and professionals, and guarantee quality through its QAP.
* B) To provide labor union representation for sheet metal workers.
* C) To manufacture high-precision digital manometers.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: A**
  * **Explanation:** NEBB is a certification and standards body that establishes TAB standards and certifies firms/individuals.

### Q10. Which of the following pieces of information is mandatory on an instrument's calibration certificate to be NEBB compliant?
* A) The manufacturer's warehouse address.
* B) The purchasing invoice price of the instrument.
* C) A statement of NIST traceability and the exact date of calibration.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: C**
  * **Explanation:** NEBB compliant certificates must state NIST traceability and the date of calibration to verify the 12-month limit.

### Q11. A supply diffuser has a design airflow of 300 CFM. If the balanced flow is 285 CFM, is this within NEBB tolerance?
* A) Yes, it is within the standard +/-10% range (270 to 330 CFM).
* B) Yes, because all individual terminals have a +/-15% tolerance.
* C) No, it falls below the minimum limit.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: A**
  * **Explanation:** 300 CFM * 0.9 = 270 CFM. 285 CFM is within the acceptable range.

### Q12. A critical laboratory exhaust fan has a design flow rate of 1000 CFM. What is the acceptable balanced range under NEBB?
* A) 800 to 1200 CFM (+/-20% of design)
* B) 1000 to 1100 CFM (+10% / 0% of design)
* C) 900 to 1100 CFM (+/-10% of design)
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** Critical airflows for containment must be balanced to positive tolerance only (+10% / 0%) to prevent reverse airflow.

### Q13. You are preparing to measure the differential pressure across a coil using a PT plug on a hydronic line carrying water at 220 F and 80 psi. What safety equipment is mandatory?
* A) Safety glasses (eye protection) and heat-resistant protective gloves.
* B) A full respirator mask and steel-toe boots only.
* C) No specialized safety gear is needed as PT plugs are self-sealing.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: A**
  * **Explanation:** High-pressure, hot water lines present severe spray/scalding risks. Eye protection and thermal gloves are mandatory when dealing with PT plugs under pressure.

### Q14. A rectangular supply duct is 24 inches wide by 12 inches high. What is the minimum grid layout required to satisfy the NEBB 6-inch maximum spacing rule?
* A) 4 x 2 grid (8 points)
* B) 4 x 3 grid (12 points)
* C) 5 x 3 grid (15 points)
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: C**
  * **Explanation:** Grid spacing must not exceed 6 inches. Width (24/6 = 4 spaces, 5 lines), Height (12/6 = 2 spaces, 3 lines). Grid: 5 x 3.

### Q15. A sterile operating room must be maintained at a positive pressure relative to the surrounding corridors. What is the primary operational reason for this system relationship?
* A) To decrease the supply fan power consumption by lowering space resistance.
* B) To force dirty air from the corridors to enter the exhaust return system of the room.
* C) To prevent infiltration of airborne pathogens and contaminants from corridors into the sterile room when doors are opened.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: C**
  * **Explanation:** Positive pressurization ensures that clean air constantly flows outward, blocking untreated air or dust from infiltrating the critical room.

### Q16. An airborne infection isolation room is balanced to -0.05 in. w.g. relative to the corridor. When the door is opened, what happens to the differential pressure and airflow direction?
* A) The airflow immediately reverses, pushing air out from the room into the corridor.
* B) The differential pressure drops to nearly 0.00 in. w.g., but the airflow direction remains inward (corridor to room) to maintain containment.
* C) The differential pressure rises significantly to -0.20 in. w.g. due to suction.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** An open door removes flow resistance, dropping static pressure to near zero. However, continuous exhaust maintains inward velocity (airflow direction) to prevent pathogen escape.

### Q17. If a return fan VFD controls are mismatched and tracking too fast (e.g., pulling 95% return flow while supply fan is at 75% flow), what envelope issues will occur?
* A) The building will become over-pressurized, pushing doors open and whistling.
* B) Exterior doors will become hard to open/close (sticking) and hot, humid outdoor air will infiltrate through cracks, risking condensation.
* C) The supply duct static pressure will immediately rise to its high limit.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** Excess return/exhaust flow relative to supply creates building-wide negative pressure, causing doors to stick and sucking in unconditioned outside air.

### Q18. Where should the duct static pressure sensor for VAV supply fan speed control be located to ensure stable and energy-efficient system operation?
* A) At the fan discharge plenum to capture the highest static pressure.
* B) Approximately 2/3 to 3/4 down the main duct trunk, prior to the final major branches.
* C) Inside the return air plenum to measure building relief air pressure.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** Placing the sensor 2/3 to 3/4 down the main trunk ensures that the furthest VAV boxes have adequate static pressure while avoiding over-pressurization near the fan.

### Q19. What is the primary operational consequence of placing a VAV duct static pressure sensor too close (e.g., within 5 feet) to the supply fan discharge?
* A) The sensor will read zero pressure because of vacuum drag.
* B) The sensor will capture high velocity pressure fluctuations and turbulence, causing the fan VFD controller to hunt and oscillate.
* C) The fan will run in reverse rotation.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** Fan discharge zones have high turbulence and dynamic pressure noise. Sensors placed here cause VFD loop instability (hunting).

### Q20. During low cooling loads, all VAV boxes are close to minimum position. The fan VFD is at its minimum speed of 15Hz. Duct static pressure rises above setpoint, and the fan enters a surging region. What is the solution?
* A) Manually close all outdoor air dampers to lower intake resistance.
* B) Double the size of the supply fan motor.
* C) Modulate a bypass relief damper installed in a bypass duct connecting the supply main back to the return plenum.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: C**
  * **Explanation:** A bypass relief damper relieves excess static pressure and maintains minimum flow through the fan during extremely low load conditions, preventing surging.
