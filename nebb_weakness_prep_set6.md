## Part 1: Management & Procedural Standards (Q1 ~ Q50)

### Q1. Under NEBB rules, what does the CP's seal on a report legally signify?
* A) That the system design complies with local fire code regulations.
* B) That the Certified Firm is financially liable for any building structural failures.
* C) That the CP has directly supervised the work and guarantees the technical accuracy of the report in accordance with NEBB standards.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: C**
  * **Explanation:** The CP's seal certifies personal responsibility for the direct supervision and technical accuracy of the report's data.

### Q2. Who holds the certificate of corporate registration with NEBB and pays the annual corporate certification fees?
* A) The individual Certified Professional (CP).
* B) The NEBB Certified Firm.
* C) The Mechanical Contractor's Association.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** Corporate registration and certification dues are the responsibility of the NEBB Certified Firm.

### Q3. Why must the calibration dates in the Instrument Log be checked against the project field testing dates?
* A) To calculate the hourly rental rate for billing.
* B) To determine the depreciation value of the tools.
* C) To verify that every measurement was taken while the instrument's calibration was active.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: C**
  * **Explanation:** Measurements taken with an expired instrument are invalid. The log proves the calibration was active during the testing window.

### Q4. If all deficiencies found during the project are resolved before the final report is compiled, what should the CP do?
* A) Omit the report certification page entirely.
* B) Note in the report that all deficiencies have been corrected, leaving the log clear.
* C) Include all resolved items in a separate billing invoice.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** A clean log or statement indicating that all deficiencies were resolved is required to show the system is in compliance.

### Q5. Who is responsible for reviewing and formally approving the TAB submittal, including traverse locations?
* A) The Controls Subcontractor.
* B) The NEBB Local Chapter.
* C) The Design Mechanical Engineer.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: C**
  * **Explanation:** The Design Engineer reviews the TAB submittals to ensure the proposed measurement locations meet design intent.

### Q6. If a fan belt is slipping and preventing the system from reaching flow, who must adjust or replace the belt?
* A) The TAB Agent.
* B) The Mechanical Contractor.
* C) The Controls Subcontractor.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** Adjusting belts, pulleys, and structural hardware is an installation maintenance task belonging to the Mechanical Contractor.

### Q7. During VAV system testing, who must configure the DDC software to force the VFDs to run at specific frequencies (e.g., 60Hz)?
* A) The Electrical Contractor.
* B) The TAB Agent.
* C) The Temperature Controls Contractor (ATC).
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: C**
  * **Explanation:** Controls contractors write the code and interface commands to force systems into test modes for the TAB agent.

### Q8. Is the TAB Agent authorized to physically resize a duct or replace a pump impeller to correct a flow deficiency?
* A) Yes, if they have the tools on site.
* B) No, the TAB Agent diagnoses the issue and documents it; physical resizing is a contractor task.
* C) Yes, under the NEBB QAP authorization.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** The TAB agent does not perform physical system redesigns or modifications. They diagnose and report deficiencies.

### Q9. Which program is offered by NEBB to guarantee the quality of a certified firm's work to the building owner?
* A) The Instrument Calibration Warranty.
* B) The Quality Assurance Program (QAP).
* C) The National Labor Agreement.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** The Quality Assurance Program (QAP) is NEBB's formal program that guarantees the work of certified firms.

### Q10. If an instrument's calibration certificate is dated January 15, 2025, when does its NEBB certification expire?
* A) January 15, 2026.
* B) July 15, 2025.
* C) January 15, 2027.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: A**
  * **Explanation:** Calibration certificates are valid for exactly 12 months (1 year) from the date of calibration.

### Q11. A return grille has a design flow of 500 CFM. If the balanced flow is 440 CFM, is this within NEBB tolerance?
* A) Yes, as long as the fan VFD frequency is at 60Hz.
* B) No, it deviates beyond the standard +/-10% range (450 to 550 CFM).
* C) Yes, because return grilles have a wider range.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** 500 CFM * 0.9 = 450 CFM. 440 CFM is outside the +/-10% range.

### Q12. An isolation room exhaust fan is designed for 1200 CFM. What is the acceptable balanced range under NEBB standards?
* A) 1200 to 1320 CFM (+10% / 0% of design)
* B) 1140 to 1260 CFM (+/-5% of design)
* C) 1080 to 1320 CFM (+/-10% of design)
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: A**
  * **Explanation:** Pressurization/containment airflows require a +10% / 0% tolerance range to maintain negative pressure.

### Q13. When inserting a probe into a PT plug on a chilled water line at 100 psi, water begins to spray out around the probe. What is the first safety action?
* A) Ignore the spray as long as it does not contact electrical boxes.
* B) Immediately back out the probe, inspect the plug's internal rubber core, and wear eye protection.
* C) Tighten the probe with pliers to force it in.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** Spraying indicates a failed plug core. Safely back out the probe while wearing protection and report the defective plug.

### Q14. A rectangular duct is 18 inches wide by 18 inches high. What is the minimum grid layout required to satisfy the NEBB 6-inch maximum spacing rule?
* A) 3 x 3 grid (9 points)
* B) 4 x 4 grid (16 points)
* C) 5 x 5 grid (25 points)
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** Width (18/6 = 3 spaces, 4 lines), Height (18/6 = 3 spaces, 4 lines). Grid: 4 x 4.

### Q15. A cleanroom packaging space is designed to maintain positive pressure. What is the direct consequence if the outside air supply fan fails while the return fan continues to run?
* A) The space pressure will remain positive due to return fan suction.
* B) The space will become negative relative to adjacent areas, causing dust infiltration.
* C) The HEPA filters will immediately rupture due to high backpressure.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** Loss of outside air supply while return/exhaust continues turns the space negative, pulling in raw, unfiltered air from surroundings.

### Q16. A negative pressure chemistry lab door is left propped open. How does this affect the laboratory's containment capability?
* A) Containment is enhanced because more corridor air dilutes the room.
* B) Containment is unaffected as long as the exhaust fan runs at 100% speed.
* C) Containment is weakened because the physical pressure barrier is gone, though air still moves inward.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: C**
  * **Explanation:** Opening the door removes the static pressure differential, weakening the barrier and making the containment susceptible to drafts, even though net air moves inward.

### Q17. During building commissioning, you find that exterior double-doors are whistling and hard to open from the inside. What fan tracking error does this indicate?
* A) Both fans are running in reverse rotation.
* B) The return fan is running too fast relative to the supply fan (under-pressurization).
* C) The supply fan is running too fast relative to the return/exhaust fans (over-pressurization).
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: C**
  * **Explanation:** Hard-to-open doors from the inside and whistling air moving outward indicate high positive pressure caused by excess supply air.

### Q18. A VAV system static pressure sensor is placed at the end of the longest duct run. What is the primary drawback of this placement?
* A) The sensor will read velocity pressure instead of static pressure.
* B) The control loop may be sluggish and prone to over-shooting due to transport delay.
* C) The fan will consume more energy than if placed at the fan discharge.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** Sensor placement at the very end of the run increases control response lag (transport delay), leading to controller overshoot and instability.

### Q19. A static pressure probe is installed in a VAV duct immediately following a 90-degree elbow. What measurement error will occur?
* A) The static pressure reading will be artificially high because of impact pressure.
* B) The sensor will read negative static pressure.
* C) Turbulent swirling air will cause highly erratic and inaccurate static pressure readings.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: C**
  * **Explanation:** Elbows generate high turbulence and swirling flow, making it impossible to measure true static pressure at that location.

### Q20. What is the physical description of fan 'surging' (stall) in a VAV system under high static, low flow conditions?
* A) The fan motor draws current exceeding its locked rotor amps.
* B) Airflow rapidly reverses and pulses through the fan blade wheel, causing severe vibration and noise.
* C) The duct collapses due to high internal vacuum.
* **Correct Answer & Explanation: B**
  * **Explanation:** Surging is an aerodynamic instability where the fan cannot overcome the high system static pressure, causing flow to stall and reverse cyclically.
